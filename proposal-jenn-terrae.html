Why Pickleball × Why Now

The same DNA,
a new court to own.

Pickleball demands exactly what BR-001 was built for — quick lateral cuts, explosive first steps, and constant transitions between offense and defense. This isn't a pivot; it's a natural extension.

Fastest-growing sport in the US & SEA

Over 48 million players in the US alone. Southeast Asia — especially Malaysia, Thailand, and Indonesia — is seeing rapid court expansion and community growth.

Hybrid Moment = pickleball DNA

Short dashes, lateral shuffles, split-step pivots — pickleball is a Hybrid Moment sport. BR-001's Bridge Mechanics™ addresses exactly this movement pattern.

Underserved market

Most players use running shoes or generic court shoes. Purpose-built pickleball footwear from legacy brands starts at $120+. There's a clear gap for a performance shoe at the right price.

Jenn & Terrae = the right partner

Deep understanding of the Southeast Asian sports market, existing pickleball community, and a curated shop aesthetic that aligns with DARIMATI's brand positioning.
